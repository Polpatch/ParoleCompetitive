# Local data

## daily-game
- Use namespaced, versioned localStorage for dated games, accepted guesses and feedback, startedAt, endedAt, status, word/schedule identity, difficulty eligibility and committed score. Also retain the current draft so a closure does not erase entered but unsubmitted letters.
- Persist each accepted guess and its terminal result as one coherent state change before visual reveal. Restoring must reconstruct keyboard knowledge, known-letter constraints and the exact next row without adding an attempt or changing time.
- Persist the game's own autocomplete choice independently from the future preference. The first accepted guess locks this choice; a completed result retains it and its committed score regardless of later settings changes.
- Preserve the draft as five ordered positions, each empty or holding one A–Z letter, so fixed letters and gaps retain their actual positions. Derive fixed positions from green feedback in accepted guesses, not from hidden solution knowledge. Retain manually entered letters and reconstruct the next editable position on restoration. With autocomplete disabled, preserve the existing sequential entry behavior.
- Restore a committed nonterminal guess without replaying its reveal, reconstructing the next draft's known correct positions when autocomplete is enabled. Preserve any manual letters already saved in that draft. Terminal games have no new active draft.
- Existing valid sessions and games without autocomplete information remain readable: default missing preference and per-game choice to disabled, preserve all accepted guesses, manual drafts, timestamps, outcomes, scores and statistics. Do not reinterpret an old game using a newly selected future preference or recompute old results with a new multiplier. This compatibility does not exempt a superseded activation-day challenge from its required withdrawal.
- Treat one browser storage origin as the device-local scope; data is not synchronized across browsers, devices or accounts. Clearing site data resets it, and private browsing may discard it.
- Reconcile competing tabs before accepting a guess; a tab with stale progress must reload the authoritative stored daily record rather than silently append a second outcome. Use a serialized write strategy for supported target browsers so a result cannot be counted twice.
- If storage is unavailable or malformed, show an honest Italian persistence warning and keep a playable in-memory session without claiming successful saving; do not silently clear unrelated site data. Document that daily enforcement and durable statistics require usable browser storage.
- Reconcile the secret-selection cutover before restoring, expiring or accepting a game. Replace only the activation date's old-schedule record with a fresh new-schedule game when that date is current: no draft, accepted rows, feedback, startedAt, endedAt, terminal result or score survive. Use the retained player preferences for the new game. Other dates' complete records and preferences remain unchanged.
- Persist a stable cutover identity/completion marker with the affected game change, under the existing serialized reconciliation discipline. Distinguish old and new challenges by schedule identity, even if their solution text happens to match. Reopening, rebuilds, repeated reconciliation and competing tabs must not erase the replacement game's progress or result, or resurrect a stale old-schedule activation-day record.
- If the cutover is first discovered after its activation date, withdraw only that date's superseded record/result before ordinary expiry reconciliation. Do not create an unplayed replacement game for that past date, invent a loss for the withdrawal, or alter intervening dates. Retain historical dictionary and schedule compatibility for dates before the secret-selection activation. Post-activation games retain their acquired selection and cannot be reconstructed from a public future schedule; the expanded allowed set remains a superset of the old set.
- A replacement game takes the retained future autocomplete preference as a new game; it inherits neither the withdrawn game's locked choice nor any fixed or manual draft letters. Subsequent reconciliation preserves the replacement game's own choice and positional draft.

- From 2026-11-08, persist the validated acquired selection with the dated game, including schema, vocabulary identity, challenge identity and solution index, coherently under the existing serialized session reconciliation. Historical validation must use the saved assignment, not recompute HMAC in the browser. Preserve existing drafts, accepted feedback, timestamps, outcome, score and locked choices on reopening.
- Apply one stable secret-selection cutover marker for 2026-11-08, replacing the abandoned 2026-11-09 v2 cutover. Withdraw only the activation day's old public record/result once, including if first discovered later; never withdraw the acquired HMAC game or a valid later day. Reject stale public-schedule writes on or after activation without clearing earlier games, preferences or a retained compatible game.
- Expire stored preceding unfinished games even when today's selection is unavailable. Do not create today's game, start time, lock game preferences or count an outcome until a valid selection is acquired. With usable current-day selection but unavailable storage, retain the existing honest in-memory fallback; without a selection, storage fallback cannot invent one.
- Retain a saved post-activation public-calendar game other than the activation-day game separately from an acquired challenge on the same date. Its presence does not prevent starting or restoring the acquired challenge. Preserve its result; expire an unfinished game at its actual day boundary under the ordinary started/unstarted rules. Reject stale public-calendar updates without deleting either retained game.

## game-preferences
- Store selected future difficulty, autocomplete and spoiler-free preferences independently from immutable completed game results. A difficulty downgrade changes the active game eligibility and future difficulty preference together.
- Autocomplete defaults to disabled and is retained across days. Before the first accepted guess, update the active game's autocomplete choice and future preference coherently; while a started game is unfinished, change neither. After conclusion, change only the future preference. Reuse the session persistence and serialized cross-tab reconciliation path rather than a separate settings-only store.

## statistics
- Derive counts, the six-bucket win distribution, total score and ordered local history from surviving saved games, deduplicated by challenge identity. Retained public-calendar and acquired challenges on the same date remain distinct and each contributes once. Reuse the existing versioned session and serialized reconciliation; no second history or preference store.
- Retain and reuse each valid concluded game's challenge identity, date, solution, accepted guesses and feedback, outcome, committed score and first/terminal timestamps for read-only history. Preserve existing valid records and immutable scores without recomputing them under later preferences. History is limited to data already retained on this browser origin; no remote retrieval, cross-device synchronization or invented missing records.
- Withdrawal removes only the superseded challenge from counts, distribution, cumulative score and history. Recompute from surviving outcomes, including zero total when none survive; do not record a negative result or clear other dates. Repeated withdrawal and stale delivery cannot remove the replacement result or restore the withdrawn record.

## result-share
- Build image and text from a snapshot of a won or lost result after its reveal, including challenge date, win/loss, accepted-guess count out of six and score. Do not include answer text, guess letters, keyboard labels, hidden DOM text, alt text with guesses, or solution metadata in the exported artifact.
- Reopening a completed current-day game restores sharing availability. Expired unfinished games have no share action. Sharing never changes progress, timestamps, score or totals.
- Tie prepared images, text and manual-copy content to the current challenge identity, not date alone. Invalidate only presentations belonging to the withdrawn challenge; late preparation or share/copy completion cannot restore its availability or trigger a new fallback. Already handed-off external shares or copies cannot be revoked.

## game-appearance
- Persist the selected appearance as system, light or dark in the existing versioned device-local session preferences, independently of difficulty, spoiler-free choice and completed outcomes. Persist the selection rather than the currently resolved system color scheme; remember it across reloads and challenge days.
- Read otherwise-valid older sessions without an appearance preference as system. Preserve their saved games, draft, difficulty, spoiler-free preference, timestamps, scores and statistics; adding the preference must not reject or reset such sessions or introduce a second runtime preference store.
- If persistence cannot be used, apply appearance choices in memory while keeping the existing honest Italian saving warning. Do not claim that the choice will survive a reload and do not clear unrelated site data. Keep the existing malformed-session failure policy for other invalid session data.
