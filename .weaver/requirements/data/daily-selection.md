# Daily selection data

## daily-word

- Vendor the existing common and 60k sources plus `paroleitaliane/660000_parole_italiane.txt` from https://github.com/napolux/paroleitaliane at revision `2951524b6d5b67986f542937cef16a54af63ff84`. Record exact source paths, acquisition date, checksums, transformations and MIT attribution. Do not import dedicated proper-name lists, `coniugazione_verbi.txt` with its separate CC-BY-SA license, or aggregates containing those datasets under an MIT-only claim.
- Retain the upstream caveat that some provenance is unknown; the list is not official, authoritative, exhaustive or legally guaranteed. Do not copy code, artwork or a schedule from the unofficial Par🇮🇹le game.
- Build one canonical vocabulary from the union of the pinned sources and documented, reviewed local additions, including AUDIO and OZONO. Preserve every previously allowed guess. Retain only exactly five ASCII letters, uppercase, deduplicate and sort reproducibly. Do not strip accents, transliterate or add input-specific bypasses. Record the additions' provenance and editorial decisions.
- Use exactly this vocabulary as both the allowed-guess set and the complete eligible-solution set. No smaller common-word solution subset remains active. Italian inflections, rare words, Italian-use loanwords and vulgar or offensive Italian terms are eligible for both uses. Reject malformed and non-Italian entries and exclusively proper names; a normal Italian meaning is sufficient even when also used as a name. Do not use initial capitalization alone as evidence of a proper name.
- Keep the current 7,679 entries unchanged. Identify this vocabulary independently of selection order by lowercase hexadecimal SHA-256 of its sorted uppercase words encoded as UTF-8, one word per LF-terminated line including a final LF. Both trusted generation and client validation use this exact identity and ordering; do not use locale-dependent sorting.
- The public selection schema is version 1 with only `schema`, `date`, `vocabularyId`, `challengeId` and `index`. `date` is a real canonical Rome date `YYYY-MM-DD`; `index` is an integer in `[0,N)`. `challengeId` is `hmac-v1:<date>:<vocabularyId>`, distinguishing the acquired challenge from any public-calendar game even if their words match. Validate all fields and mutual consistency before adopting the record; schema, date or vocabulary mismatches never fall back to a public selection.
- Retain a validated acquired record in the existing versioned local session together with its game, not in an unrelated state store. It is sufficient to restore that same day's solution and validate its saved game without any secret or network. Preserve earlier dated games and outcomes. Do not clear a valid current record on fetch failure or replace its assignment with a conflicting response.
- Keep the v1 epoch, ordering, algorithm and identity for historical dates before 2026-11-08. Retain pinned sources, additions, exclusions and provenance in the repository; no source downloads during gameplay. Remove the active frozen v2 future calendar and its generation/runtime path: the abandoned 2026-11-09 activation is not a second migration. Do not bundle a public post-activation selection sequence, seed or equivalent.

## daily-publication

- Persist fixed assignments append-only on the dedicated `daily-data` branch, keyed by canonical Rome date, with the exact public record and its vocabulary identity. Store only dates already reached, never a future sequence. The durable branch is data, not executable workflow or script input. It survives Pages failures, artifact expiry and code rebuilds; do not use Actions caches or expiring artifacts as the source of truth.
- Reserve an absent current-day record once before making it public on Pages. Existing records take precedence over recomputation and must never be overwritten, including after a key change, vocabulary change or failed deployment. Recover by reusing their exact content; if an existing record is malformed or incompatible with the deploying code, stop with an actionable error rather than delete or regenerate it.
- Deploy only the current record as `daily/current.json` within the complete Pages artifact. Older fixed records may remain publicly visible in branch history; no future record, HMAC digest, key or equivalent secret material is added to the branch or artifact. Do not publish historical days that were never fixed as a side effect of recovery.
- Freeze a published day's vocabulary identity and selection across code releases. A vocabulary change needs an explicitly agreed later boundary and compatible client release; it cannot reinterpret an already fixed index. Secret rotation similarly needs an explicit prospective period, never a silent replacement.
