# Statistics and match history

## statistics
- At the top of the Statistiche tab, show two bordered cards side by side for wins and losses. Center a trophy and a broken-heart icon respectively over the upper border, interrupting the border behind each icon. Center each count within its card; provide the Italian names Vittorie and Sconfitte accessibly, not through icons alone.
- Below the cards show Punteggio totale, the cumulative score. Below that render a genuine six-column vertical histogram of winning attempt counts: horizontal labels 1–6, counts visible for every bucket, a common linear vertical scale from zero to the maximum count, and an accessible description of the scale. Equal counts have equal heights; a zero count has no positive-height bar. With all buckets zero, show the labels, zero counts and an Italian no-wins state, without division by zero or fabricated bars. Do not imply an online ranking.
- Keep the cards, labels, icons, chart and counts readable at 320 px and desktop widths, in light and dark appearance and with enlarged text. Preserve non-color access to all values.

## game-interface/Statistics open
- Use one existing statistics modal with accessible tabs Statistiche and Storico. Each manual opening and automatic recap selects Statistiche; each fresh opening starts with all history entries collapsed. Tabs expose their selected state and associated panels, support standard keyboard tab navigation, visible focus and screen-reader names, and never send input to the game.
- In Statistiche, place the victory/loss cards, total score and histogram first, followed by today's outcome, fixed score and duration when its win/loss has been revealed. Before that reveal, omit today's result summary and share action, preserving the previous visible statistics. Condividi risultato and preparation/status/manual-copy presentation appear only in this tab and only for the applicable current-day result; no historical share action is added.
- Keep the share footer outside the scrollable information area and fully reserve its height whenever visible. Scrolling must bring the final item and manual-copy text entirely above the footer. Storico has no sharing footer or share feedback.
- Show history as independently expandable accordions, ordered by conclusion newest first with stable ties and no pagination. The closed row contains only the solution word, accepted-attempt count and committed points, with accessible labels for those values. Different challenges on the same date remain distinct entries even if their visible summary matches. Every entry starts closed, and opening another never closes existing entries.
- Expanded content shows a static six-by-five board with every accepted guess letter and its saved green/yellow/gray feedback; unused cells remain neutral. Show the challenge date, outcome (Vittoria, Sconfitta or Scaduta), accepted-attempt count, committed points and fixed solving duration. Use non-color descriptions for tiles. There is no editable draft, keyboard, guess submission, replay or repeated reveal animation inside history.
- History is intentionally letter-bearing on this device; spoiler-free sharing does not hide history letters or change export privacy. Duration uses recorded first and terminal submissions, or the original expiry boundary, never the animation completion time: m:ss below one hour and h:mm:ss thereafter, truncating fractional seconds for display only. A first-guess win shows 0:00; do not show start/end clock times or alter score precision.
- Explain an empty history in Italian and retain the device-local-data explanation: only matches still saved in this browser are available, with no synchronization or recovery of cleared data. Do not invent missing entries. Unfinished/unstarted/withdrawn records cannot expose solution words through history.
- During the same modal opening, switching tabs preserves valid accordion expansions. Theme changes and ordinary day/storage rerenders preserve the selected tab and valid expansions, with usable focus and scrolling. A new locally submitted terminal reveal selects Statistiche once under the existing recap rules. Removal of a withdrawn entry discards only its expansion and moves focus to a usable nearby history control if needed, without closing the modal or restoring stale content.
- Automatically open once after the final feedback reveal of a newly won/lost game submitted in this tab. Immediately replace any other open modal. Do not automatically open for a reload, restored result, another tab's result, dismissed recap, nonterminal row, day expiry or withdrawn challenge. A late reveal after a day/challenge change cannot open an obsolete recap.
- Manual opening after restoring today's completed game includes the same fixed duration, score, statistics and sharing action. Day change keeps the selected tab open, refreshes history/totals and removes the previous day's current-result summary and sharing action. Vocabulary replacement keeps the modal usable, removes the withdrawn match, outcome, duration, score and sharing/manual-copy/status presentation and refreshes totals from surviving outcomes, including delayed discovery after activation; only the replacement's own revealed win/loss supplies a new current recap.
