# Game interface

## General
- User-facing interface language is Italian; title is ParoleCompetitive. Use a restrained responsive layout for phone and desktop: centered grid, keyboard beneath, reachable settings and statistics, completed result and share action. No reference image or custom brand artwork is required.
- Provide a short Italian explanation of six guesses, duplicate-letter feedback, daily reset in Europe/Rome, time starting with the first accepted guess, local-only data and the difference between standard and hard rules.
- Use semantic buttons, visible keyboard focus, descriptive accessible names, live feedback for rejection/result, and sufficient contrast. Color is not the sole accessible indicator of tile state. No separate high-contrast setting is required.

## daily-game
- Always render six rows of five tiles, never the five-row layout from the initial request. Show the active draft and retain all accepted rows.
- Provide an embedded A–Z keyboard in exactly three centered, non-wrapping rows: QWERTYUIOP; ASDFGHJKL; then Invio, ZXCVBNM and Cancella. Preserve these letter groups and order on phone and desktop, including a 320 px viewport without horizontal page scrolling. Inset the shorter rows and make action keys wider where space permits; do not add digits, punctuation or accent keys.
- Physical A–Z, Enter and Backspace have equivalent game behavior and retain native operating-system key repeat; do not hijack typing in other editable controls or shortcuts with modifiers, and do not send game input while a modal panel is open.
- With autocomplete enabled, show known correct letters in their exact positions in the active draft as green fixed cells. Accessible descriptions identify the letter, position, correctness and fixed status; color alone is insufficient. Other draft cells remain neutral. Entry and deletion skip fixed cells with sequential editing of the remaining cells; no manual cell selection is added. Fixed cells and their descriptions remain readable in both light and dark appearance.
- Show newly prefilled cells only after the complete preceding row reveal, simultaneously with the next draft becoming editable. Before completion, show no prefilled next row from the new feedback. Do not show an active next draft for a terminal game. Restoration displays the reconstructed draft immediately without replaying the accepted row reveal.
- On each accepted submission, reveal only that row with tile rotations ordered from left to right: gray absent, yellow misplaced, green correct. Each rotation lasts 1 s; starts are staggered by 0.3 s at 0, 0.3, 0.6, 0.9 and 1.2 s from the start of the row reveal. Rotations intentionally overlap; the full sequence ends when the fifth rotation finishes, nominally at 2.2 s.
- Each tile remains neutral, with no feedback in its accessible description, until the midpoint of its own rotation. At that midpoint, reveal its feedback color and matching accessible description together; keep that feedback visible afterward, while later tiles reveal in turn. Do not expose the entire row's feedback at once. This submitted-row reveal rule also applies to letters that were fixed and green in the editable draft before submission.
- Prevent repeat submits, edits and new guesses until the fifth rotation finishes. Keep cumulative keyboard feedback, visible statistics, result messaging and sharing availability at their pre-reveal presentation until the whole sequence finishes, then present the committed outcome. The accepted guess and any terminal result remain committed before animation, without extending solving time or changing score.
- With the system reduced-motion preference enabled, reveal all five feedback colors and matching accessible descriptions immediately after the accepted guess is committed, with no rotations or artificial delays. Complete the same reveal-dependent updates and input unlocking order immediately; terminal games remain locked.
- Keyboard keys show cumulative best feedback green > yellow > gray, and wholly absent keys are visibly and functionally disabled in both modes. Enter and delete remain usable when appropriate.
- Explain invalid length, unknown word, absent-letter use and hard-mode violations without spending a guess. A completed game shows win/loss and its fixed score; a day transition explains the reset and shows the new empty game. No solution letters appear in share artifacts even if the live result shows the answer.
- Explain the secret-selection cutover in Italian when it withdraws a saved activation-day game: that day's challenge and result were replaced, while earlier results and settings were retained. Show the replacement's empty six-by-five board, fresh keyboard knowledge and unlocked eligible input; no old reveal, score or result message may reappear. A delayed cutover discovery explains the withdrawn day without presenting a new game for that past day.

- On the embedded keyboard, show an immediate static pressed border and inset shadow while an enabled key is held. Preserve its green/yellow/gray knowledge color, visible focus and sufficient contrast in both appearances; do not animate or move the key. Disabled keys do not acquire actionable pressed feedback.
- A pointer press on any enabled embedded key, including Invio and Cancella, performs its normal action immediately. The first repeated action is at 1000 ms of continuous holding, then every 150 ms. A short press produces exactly one action; release and its resulting click produce no additional action. Preserve semantic keyboard and assistive-technology button activation without duplicate actions.
- Keep at most one held embedded key active. Ordinary draft or appearance rendering must neither cancel the hold nor create additional repeat streams. Stop on release, pointer leaving the key, pointer cancellation or capture loss, window/focus loss, hidden page, modal opening, day/challenge change, key becoming disabled, or accepted-row reveal starting. Never resume after such a stop without a new press. Every activation respects current game eligibility, absent keys, autocomplete, reveal and terminal locks; queued old actions cannot affect a replacement challenge.
- Suppress text selection and touch callout on embedded keys only. Keep guide, other explanatory text and manual-copy text selectable. Page zoom restrictions are specified on game-interface; do not disable all page gestures merely to suppress key selection.
- Use short, specific Italian feedback: incomplete/non-A–Z input "Servono 5 lettere dalla A alla Z"; fixed-letter violation "Le lettere verdi sono fisse"; excluded-letter rejection "Lettera già esclusa"; dictionary rejection "Parola non ammessa"; hard-mode rejection "Rispetta gli indizi della modalità difficile"; cross-tab conflict "Partita aggiornata in un’altra scheda. Controlla la griglia". Preserve the distinct rejection reasons and existing no-attempt/no-clock effects.
- After a nonterminal accepted row's complete reveal, announce "Tentativo inviato" accessibly without a visible success toast. Reveal-dependent messaging remains locked until the existing full reveal completes, including the immediate reduced-motion path. Final copy is "Hai indovinato! Punteggio: {score}" or "Tentativi finiti. La parola era {solution}. Punteggio: 0"; retain it in the statistics recap and allow brief toast feedback only after the terminal reveal. Keep solutions out of share artifacts.

## game-preferences
- Settings expose hard mode and spoiler-free sharing. Explain observed-duplicate minimums, the 10% hard bonus and losing that bonus on downgrade. Disable an in-game standard→hard switch with an explanation, but allow pre-game and post-game changes in both directions. Spoiler-free remains editable.
- Add a labeled Autocomplete toggle in the settings modal, off by default. Explain in Italian that every previously found green is filled in the next attempt, cannot be erased or overwritten, and is skipped when typing or deleting. Explain the ×0,98 score factor, its application from the first accepted guess even without prefilled letters, and its multiplication with the eligible hard bonus before one final rounding.
- Disable the Autocomplete toggle after the first accepted guess while the game remains unfinished, with an accessible explanation. Rejected guesses leave it editable. Re-enable it after conclusion and explain that changes then apply to future games only, without altering the completed result. Reflect the retained preference on reopening. Keep the modal, focus and scrolling usable when this choice changes, and preserve the existing appearance and spoiler-free controls.

## statistics
- Show wins, losses, a labeled distribution of wins in one through six guesses, and the latest game's score. Correctly render the empty state and zero-height buckets without implying an online ranking.
