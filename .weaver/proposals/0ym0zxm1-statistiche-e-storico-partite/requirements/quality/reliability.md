# Reliability and privacy

## General
- No backend, authentication, analytics, tracking, external runtime fonts or application cloud services. After static assets and a valid current-day selection have been acquired, that day's gameplay requires no network. New days after secret-selection activation require acquisition of their published selection; no future offline autonomy is promised. No PWA, service worker, install flow or promised cold-start offline support.
- Support current stable Chromium, Firefox and Safari, including mobile layouts; do not require native Web Share to play. Use platform feature detection for optional sharing and clipboard features.
- Keyboard operation, focus visibility, screen-reader feedback and reduced motion must work. Exercise the actual narrow-screen and desktop surface, not only unit tests.

## daily-game
- Deterministic rule tests cover duplicate-letter allocation, a winning sixth guess, six incorrect guesses, rejection without spending a guess or starting time, hard-mode observed multiplicity, gray-surplus keys, score deltas/stagnation, first-guess t=0 and time across closure.
- Include boundaries for difficult-mode downgrade and 1.10 bonus applied before a single final rounding. Browser smoke must exercise real input, a reveal, reload restoration, terminal locking and a day rollover using controlled time/data rather than waiting for midnight.
- Browser verification must observe the accepted row's left-to-right 0.3 s start offsets and 1 s rotations, per-tile color and accessible feedback at each rotation's midpoint, and input locking through the fifth rotation's completion at 2.2 s. Verify that cumulative keyboard feedback, visible statistics, result messaging and sharing do not expose the new outcome before the complete reveal, and that reduced motion shows feedback immediately without rotation or artificial waiting. Preserve submission-time timestamps and score, committed-guess restoration without replay, and terminal locking.
- Verify autocomplete with noncontiguous green positions, duplicate green letters and greens accumulated across accepted rows. Exercise typing and Backspace with both keyboard surfaces: fixed letters cannot be replaced or deleted, empty positions determine completeness, and rejection preserves manual letters and fixed positions. Autocomplete off preserves existing input and hard rules.
- Check ×0.98 on a first-guess win and a game with no earlier usable green; check ×1.078 for eligible hard plus autocomplete, ×0.98 after hard downgrade, and zero for loss or expiry. Use cases that distinguish single final rounding from intermediate rounding. Settings toggles and rejected guesses before the first accepted guess must not lock autocomplete or alter results.
- Browser smoke must observe green fixed cells and their accessible descriptions only after the entire prior reveal, correct sequential cursor skipping, reduced-motion behavior, and no next draft after a terminal result. Reload must retain a partly entered positional draft without replaying reveals or changing time, guesses or score. Loading valid pre-autocomplete records must preserve progress, manual drafts, results and scores with autocomplete disabled, except for the existing required withdrawal of a superseded activation-day challenge.
- Verify cross-tab reconciliation and exactly-once outcomes. Do not add arbitrary retries, telemetry or anti-cheat mechanisms.
- Add real-dictionary regression coverage through the production admission path for AUDIO and OZONO in fresh games, case normalization, and an actually absent five-letter word. Assert accepted feedback and clock/attempt transitions, and continued rejection for known-letter or hard-mode violations rather than only checking list lengths.
- Verify activation-day withdrawal of unstarted, playing, won, lost and already-expired old-schedule records, including first discovery after activation. Other dates and preferences stay intact. Repeated open/reconcile, reload, competing tabs and stale old-schedule records cannot erase or resurrect the replacement game or count a withdrawal as a loss.
- Browser smoke exercises actual input with the real dictionary and the cutover using controlled calendar/storage: reset notice, empty board, restored earlier statistics, correct share locking, and persistence of the replacement game's new progress after reload. Keep all existing reveal timing, reduced-motion and terminal-locking behavior.
- Exercise replacement during an autocomplete reveal and with a partly entered positional draft. Confirm that no withdrawn fixed/manual letters or late reveal updates enter the replacement, that its choice comes from the retained future preference, and that repeated reconciliation preserves its own choice and progress.

- On the actual embedded keyboard verify single short activation, no release/click duplicate, first repeat no earlier than 1000 ms and 150 ms cadence thereafter for a letter, Cancella and rejected Invio. Verify repeat survival across ordinary draft rerenders, one active hold, full-draft no-op and deletion skipping fixed greens. An accepted Invio stops the hold before reveal and cannot resubmit after unlocking. Exercise release, leaving the key, cancellation/capture loss, blur, hidden page, modal opening, disabled key and day/challenge replacement; no queued activation leaks into the new context. Confirm no key-label selection/callout, preserved physical and assistive activation, static pressed feedback and unaffected selectable manual text.

## game-preferences
- Exercise autocomplete changes before play, after rejected submissions, during a started unfinished game and after conclusion. Verify that in-game changes are refused without changing the current choice or future preference; post-game changes affect only future games. Reopening and the next day retain the correct respective choices, including after cross-tab reconciliation. Verify the settings label, disabled explanation, score-factor explanation and keyboard accessibility on the real interface.

## result-share
- Verify native image capability, text fallback, clipboard denial/manual copying, cancellation and generic failure. Exercise the actual export and inspect its pixels or drawn content: no guess/solution letters, and a completely neutral first row with spoiler-free enabled. Do not rely on a test that merely checks that a sharing API was called.
- Preserve native share user activation: prepare the result image before the share gesture where necessary; do not lose activation during asynchronous rasterization. Observe actual behavior in a supporting browser/device when available and document any device-specific verification limit without claiming delivery. Describe available automated tests accurately; do not claim pixel-level or preparation-retry coverage unless those checks exist in the checked-in suite.
- Verify that vocabulary replacement removes the old result's share action, prepared artifact and visible manual-copy text; delayed preparation or completion cannot re-enable it or start a fallback. The new challenge enables sharing only after its own completed result reveal. Do not claim revocation of external shares/copies already handed off.

## game-appearance
- Exercise the real interface in system/light/dark modes, including a system change while open and both manual overrides. Verify readable tiles, keys, disabled controls, focus, dialogs and sharing feedback in both resolved palettes.
- Verify appearance restoration and an otherwise-valid legacy session with no appearance preference without changing saved progress, scores or statistics. Exercise unavailable storage and confirm that the in-memory appearance still works with the persistence warning.

## game-interface
- Exercise all three header triggers, accessible names and modal titles with pointer and keyboard. Verify focus containment, all three dismissal routes, focus return, inert background, preserved draft and continuing solving time. Updating appearance or preparing a share must not drop focus out of the dialog.
- Exercise the actual 320 px and desktop surfaces: three stable QWERTY rows with usable action keys and no horizontal overflow; long settings/guide/result content must scroll within the modal. Keep the share footer visible and prove the last information item and manual-copy text can be brought fully above it without being covered.
- Exercise automatic recap after a locally submitted win and loss only after the terminal reveal, including reduced motion and replacing another open modal. Closing, rerendering, reloading or observing another tab's result must not reopen it; a day change must suppress a stale reveal and remove an old result/share action from an open statistics modal. Confirm fixed duration and score, including 0:00 for a first-guess win, without double-counting an outcome.
- Verify a same-day vocabulary replacement keeps the current modal open and usable, removes the withdrawn result, duration, share action and obsolete manual-copy feedback, and refreshes statistics from surviving outcomes. A late reveal for the withdrawn challenge must not open or repopulate a recap; only the replacement game's own locally submitted terminal reveal can do so. Exercise replacement during a reveal and with statistics already open, including reduced motion.
- Update affected existing browser smoke flows for icon triggers and modal-only sharing. Document the delivered appearance choices, popup behavior, final duration and sharing entry point in the English README and changelog; accurately describe exercised verification.

- Verify the real game view at 320×568, 360×640, larger phone and desktop viewports with browser chrome/safe-area constraints: complete six-row board and three-row keyboard, reachable header actions and no page overflow at standard text size. Exercise smaller height/landscape and enlarged text with accessible scrolling rather than clipping, plus persistent warning and unavailable/retry states. Confirm the body introduction/footer moved into the guide and existing settings/statistics explanations remain.
- Verify double-tap and pinch suppression on a real touch-capable browser/device when available, not merely viewport attributes or synthetic clicks; record browser-enforced overrides and any device verification limit. Do not claim OS/browser magnification can be disabled. Confirm manual-copy text remains selectable.
- Exercise toast replacement, consecutive-duplicate suppression during held Invio, 4-second expiry, hover/focus pause, manual dismissal, and no timer reset or duplicate announcement on rerender. Observe position and contrast in light/dark appearance, keyboard focus and polite screen-reader feedback without input interruption. Verify one accessible presentation moves into/out of each modal without losing lifetime, focus or scroll; share cancellation stays quiet and manual-copy text, persistent risks/acquisition status and recap survive toast expiry. Day/withdrawal changes remove obsolete feedback and stale asynchronous replies cannot restore it. Preserve reveal timing, automatic recap and reduced-motion behavior. Update the affected English README and changelog with mobile interaction/zoom limitations and toast behavior and describe only exercised verification.
