# Panels and result sharing

## result-share
- Enable sharing only for a won/lost game after its feedback reveal. Export a clean result card image, not the whole live page: title, date, outcome/attempt count, score and a six-by-five grid. Unused cells are neutral. No letters, solution or keyboard.
- With spoiler-free enabled, the entire first row is neutral, including green/yellow/gray information; with it disabled, preserve first-row feedback colors but still omit letters. Apply the same concealment to the text fallback, using neutral placeholders instead of colored cells in its first row.
- Show meaningful Italian success, failure or manual-copy feedback. Native cancellation is quiet and never triggers clipboard copying or a PNG download. No automatic PNG download fallback.
- When the displayed result belongs to the withdrawn challenge, disable sharing and remove its obsolete manual-copy text and share-status feedback. Keep sharing disabled until the replacement game reaches a revealed won/lost result.
- The only Condividi risultato action is in the statistics/result modal's bottom footer, available for the current day's revealed win/loss and absent for unfinished or expired games. Remove the duplicate game-page action. Keep the footer visible outside the scrolling information area; reserve its full height so every item, including the final item, can scroll entirely above it rather than underneath it.
- Keep preparation, success, failure and manual-copy feedback within the modal. Present brief completion/failure feedback as the single current toast inside the modal, following game-interface toast behavior; preparation state and manual-copy guidance remain persistent. Place selectable manual-copy text in its scrollable area, reachable with keyboard and touch without obscuring it with the footer. Preserve existing capability detection, preparation/retry behavior and native user activation. Application appearance changes do not restyle the exported image or text.

- Use concise Italian feedback: preparation "Preparo il risultato…"; preparation failure "Preparazione non riuscita. Riprova"; capability-check failure "Condivisione non disponibile. Riprova"; share failure "Condivisione non riuscita. Riprova"; native completion "Condivisione completata" without promising delivery; clipboard success "Risultato copiato"; manual fallback "Copia il testo qui sotto". Native cancellation stays silent. Keep selectable manual text and its guidance visible until no longer applicable, independent of toast expiry.

## game-appearance
- Add an Aspetto control to the settings modal with the Italian choices Sistema, Chiaro and Scuro. Start with Sistema when no choice is saved; visibly identify the selected preference, not just the resolved light/dark appearance.
- Apply selection changes immediately to the whole live interface, including panels, backdrop, board, keyboard, disabled controls, focus indicators, notices and sharing footer. With Sistema selected, immediately follow changes to the device's preferred color scheme; manual Chiaro and Scuro override those changes.
- Preserve the current light appearance. Use a neutral very-dark-gray background with slightly lighter surfaces for dark appearance. Maintain readable text, distinct controls and visible focus in both palettes, preserving green/yellow/gray meanings and non-color tile descriptions. Do not add a separate high-contrast mode.
- Changing appearance does not close the open modal, reset focus or scrolling unnecessarily, alter the game or its score, or redesign share artifacts.

## game-interface
- Replace the settings, playing guide and statistics accordions with three semantic icon buttons at the top right of the header: gear for Impostazioni, question mark for Come si gioca, and chart for Statistiche. Supply descriptive Italian accessible names; keep all three reachable on narrow screens.
- Open one centered modal at a time, with an accessible title, an explicit Chiudi button, a dimmed backdrop and a height constrained to the viewport. Its content scrolls vertically on phone and desktop. Keep background content inert and prevent background scrolling while open.
- Move focus into the modal, contain keyboard focus within it and close on Chiudi, Escape or a click/tap directly on the backdrop, never from interacting inside the panel. Return focus to its opener; after automatic result opening return focus to the Statistiche icon. Do not let modal keystrokes reach the game.
- Preserve the current draft and the running solving clock when opening or closing panels. Retain the same panel during preference changes, appearance changes, result preparation and ordinary game/day/storage rerenders; keep its contents current and focus usable. A reload begins without an open panel.
- Keep the existing complete guide, local-data explanations, difficulty rules and spoiler-free controls in their respective modals. Do not change difficulty locks, results, scores or statistic counting.

- At standard text size, fit the main game view without vertical or horizontal page scrolling in portrait CSS viewports at least 320 px wide and 568 px high: header and its three actions, date, all six five-tile rows and all three keyboard rows remain visible and usable. Size against the currently visible viewport, including browser chrome and safe-area insets; adapt spacing and board size rather than clipping content or hiding rows. Keep stable keyboard row groups and usable action keys. On smaller heights, landscape or enlarged text, allow accessible page scrolling rather than clipping controls or reducing content to unreadable sizes. Modal content keeps its existing internal scrolling and visible sharing footer.
- Remove the introductory sentence and device-local-data footer from the main game body. Put the short introduction and the full local-data explanation in Come si gioca: data stays in this browser/device, is not synchronized, and clearing site data can erase progress; private browsing may discard it. Retain the existing data explanations in settings and statistics and the complete guide. Do not remove active persistence/coordinating warnings.
- Request suppression of double-tap and pinch page zoom throughout the page, including open panels, where the browser permits it. The developer accepts the accessibility cost of disabling voluntary pinch zoom. Browser/OS-enforced magnification and user overrides are outside application control; do not claim universal prevention, interfere with those overrides, or clip enlarged text. Keyboard selection suppression must not prevent manual copying.
- Render brief gameplay rejections, cross-tab/day/replacement notices, terminal feedback and share completion/failure as toast feedback. Keep acquisition/loading/incompatibility status and retry/reload actions, persistence and multi-tab risk warnings, final statistics recap and manual-copy guidance persistent while applicable. Do not turn persistent warnings into disappearing-only notifications. Preserve date and retained-data information in replacement notices.
- Show one toast at a time, replacing the previous feedback without a queue; dismiss after 4 seconds of active display, pausing while hovered or keyboard-focused, and provide an accessible Chiudi action. Deduplicate consecutive identical feedback so held Invio does not flood visible messages or live announcements. Toast display never steals focus, opens a panel or changes the draft, timing, guesses or score. Dismissal keeps focus usable. Ordinary rerenders do not restart the timeout or reannounce the same notification.
- Position the toast below the header in an upper overlay without covering keyboard keys, header actions, retry/reload controls or share actions. While any modal is open, present the sole toast inside that modal, not behind the inert backdrop or in a second global copy. Preserve current feedback and remaining lifetime across opening/closing a panel; keep focus and modal scroll stable. Announce transient feedback once through a polite live region without duplicate inline announcements. Clear challenge-specific obsolete feedback on day change or withdrawal and reject stale asynchronous completions; a current reset/replacement notice may replace it.
- Use short Italian acquisition and warning copy without losing meaning: "Carico la sfida…", "Sfida di oggi non disponibile. Riprova più tardi", "Aggiorna il gioco per questa sfida", "Salvataggio non disponibile: i progressi possono andare persi e la sfida giornaliera non è garantita", "Usa una sola scheda per evitare conflitti". Keep Riprova and Ricarica pagina when required. Explain day rollover as "Nuovo giorno, nuova sfida"; retain explicit unavailability when the new selection is not acquired. A cutover notice identifies the affected date and says its challenge/result were replaced while previous results/settings remain; if no replacement selection is acquired, explain that the new challenge is pending rather than claiming a playable game.
