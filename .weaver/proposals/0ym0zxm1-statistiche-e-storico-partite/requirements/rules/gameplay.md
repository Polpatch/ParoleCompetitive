# Daily puzzle rules

## daily-game
- Exactly six guesses, each exactly five A–Z letters. Normalize case only; reject accented letters, nonletters, proper names and words absent from the allowed dictionary. Every solution must be an allowed guess.
- Rejected or incomplete submissions consume no guess, do not start or reset time, and explain the rejection in Italian. A correct sixth guess wins; only a sixth incorrect accepted guess loses.
- Evaluate duplicates in two passes: assign greens for exact positions and consume those occurrences, then assign yellows left-to-right from the remaining solution occurrences; all unmatched occurrences are gray. Never award more positive occurrences of a letter than the solution contains.
- Cumulative keyboard knowledge uses green > yellow > gray and never downgrades. Disable letters proved wholly absent in both standard and hard mode, on both on-screen and physical keyboards. A gray surplus duplicate does not disable a letter with positive evidence. Never use unrevealed solution knowledge to disable a key.
- Hard mode adds fixed green positions, inclusion of observed yellow letters outside every position already excluded by yellow feedback, and a minimum occurrence count equal to the maximum green-plus-yellow count for that letter within any one prior accepted row. Do not sum rows, infer unseen duplicates, or enforce a secret upper bound. Gray-only letters are already blocked in both modes.
- With autocomplete enabled, each next playable draft contains the union of green positions from all prior accepted guesses, with their exact letters. Green occurrences are tracked by position, including duplicate letters. Do not fill yellow positions, infer additional occurrences or consult unrevealed solution information. The first draft has no automatically filled letters.
- Autocomplete-fixed letters cannot be overwritten or deleted in either standard or hard mode. Manual letters fill the remaining positions from left to right, skipping fixed positions. Backspace removes the rightmost manually entered letter and returns the entry position to that cell, skipping fixed positions; when there are no manual letters it changes nothing. A full draft accepts no further letters. No mouse or arrow-key cell selection is required. Without autocomplete, existing input and hard-mode validation remain unchanged.
- An incomplete submission with any empty position is rejected without disturbing the draft. The word submitted for validation is the five positions in order, including fixed letters. Autocomplete does not bypass dictionary, absent-letter, hard-mode, duplicate-letter or guess-limit rules.
- New fixed positions become visible only after the entire accepted row has been revealed and only if another attempt remains playable; reduced motion performs the same update immediately. No next playable draft is created after a win, loss or expiry. Filling a draft never submits it, consumes a guess or changes timestamps.
- The first accepted submission fixes startedAt. A terminal accepted submission fixes endedAt immediately, before animation. elapsed seconds t=max(0,(endedAt-startedAt)/1000), including hidden-tab time and browser closure. The initial correct guess therefore has t=0. Rejections and page reopening do not reset startedAt.
- For each accepted row, G and Y count greens and yellows. With prior counts initially zero, ΔG=max(0,G-currentPreviousG), ΔY=max(0,Y-currentPreviousY). Set s=0 if either delta is positive, otherwise increment the consecutive stagnation count s (initially zero). Regressing or unchanged counts with neither positive delta are stagnation. The first stagnating row has no penalty; later consecutive rows pay 50·(s−1) each.
- B=1000+Σ over accepted rows of (100·ΔG+40·ΔY−50·max(0,s−1)). For a win in n accepted guesses, raw=max(0,B)/(n·(1+t/60)). Score=Math.round(raw·m·a), with m=1.10 only if the game started hard and never downgraded, otherwise m=1; a=0.98 if autocomplete was enabled at the first accepted submission, otherwise a=1. The combined multiplier is 1.078 for an eligible hard game with autocomplete. Round once, after both multipliers, never once per multiplier. A loss or expiry scores zero.
- The first accepted guess locks the game's autocomplete choice and, when enabled, incurs its multiplier even for a first-guess victory or when no letter has been automatically filled. Rejected guesses and toggles before the first accepted guess incur no multiplier and do not lock the choice. Later preference changes cannot remove a committed game's multiplier or alter its score.
- A submitted guess and its outcome are committed once, before the reveal; prevent duplicate Invio, edits and new guesses during the reveal. Ignore guesses after any terminal result. Reduced-motion presentation preserves the same commit and unlocking order.
- A started unfinished game expires at day change with endedAt at the actual Europe/Rome day boundary, score zero and one loss. An unstarted game expires without a result. A missed boundary discovered later must not extend yesterday's play.
- Dictionary admission uses the same complete canonical set as solution eligibility; membership does not bypass known-gray or hard-mode restrictions. AUDIO and OZONO are accepted with ordinary feedback in an otherwise eligible fresh game, regardless of input case; an absent word remains rejected without an attempt or clock change.
- Withdrawing an activation-day challenge is neither a win, loss nor ordinary expiry. Even a previously won or lost challenge is removed from active play and gives no surviving result; its replacement begins with six guesses and no solving time until its first accepted submission. Earlier completed games remain immutable.
- Withdrawal also discards all fixed and manual letters of the superseded challenge. Its delayed reveal cannot populate the replacement's draft. The replacement starts without known correct positions and takes the retained future autocomplete preference; repeated reconciliation cannot reset its own progress or locked choice.

## game-preferences
- Defaults: standard difficulty, autocomplete disabled and spoiler-free sharing enabled. Persist preferences across days.
- Before the first accepted guess, changing difficulty in either direction is allowed. Once a game has started, hard→standard is allowed and irrevocably forfeits that game's hard bonus; standard→hard is forbidden. A rejected guess does not lock difficulty.
- After a game has ended, either difficulty can be selected for the next game; the completed game's difficulty, score and statistics remain unchanged. A new day's game takes the then-current preference.
- Autocomplete is independent of difficulty and may be changed in either direction only before the first accepted guess or after conclusion. Before play, change the current game and future preference without changing manual draft letters; while a started game is unfinished, reject changes to both. After conclusion, change only the future preference. Each new game, including a same-day replacement challenge, takes the then-current autocomplete preference; reopening an existing game retains that game's choice. Rejected guesses do not lock autocomplete.
- Spoiler-free can change at any time and affects only subsequent sharing, never the game score or previous outcomes.

## statistics
- Count each daily terminal outcome at most once, across refreshes, reopening and multiple tabs. Wins increment total wins and the matching one-through-six guess bucket. Exhausted guesses and started expiries increment total losses. Unstarted expiry changes nothing.
- Total score is the sum of immutable committed scores of all surviving counted outcomes, once per challenge; losses and started expiries contribute zero. Display zero with no outcomes. Replace the aggregate latest-score display with this cumulative total, retaining today's own score in its result recap. No ranking, online leaderboard or extra streak system is introduced.
- Client clocks and local results are trusted; do not claim authoritative timing, anti-cheat, tamper resistance or cross-device synchronization.
- Recompute counts, distribution, total score and history from surviving outcomes on withdrawal. Removing a win removes its bucket and score; removing a loss removes that loss, not an extra score. Repeated withdrawal and delayed superseded outcomes cannot disturb the replacement result.
- History contains wins, exhausted-guess losses and started expiries, including today's won/lost challenge only after its full reveal. Exclude unstarted expiry, unfinished and withdrawn challenges. Revealed/restored completed records remain readable without replay or mutation.
- Order history by terminal timestamp descending; break ties by challenge date descending and then challenge identity in ascending lexical order for stable presentation. Keep different challenges on the same date distinct. Show all retained eligible records without pagination.
- Each attempt count is the number of accepted guesses, not six for an early expiry. Historical duration is max(0, terminal timestamp minus first accepted submission), with expiry ending at its original day boundary. No replay, historical sharing or recalculation of scores is introduced.
