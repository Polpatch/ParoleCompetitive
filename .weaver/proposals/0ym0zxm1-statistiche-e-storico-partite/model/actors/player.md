# Player

A person playing the daily challenge and managing the presentation of their results.

## Events

### OPENED
The player opens or returns to the game.

### GUESS_SUBMITTED
The player submits the current guess.

### DIFFICULTY_SELECTED
The player selects standard or hard difficulty.

### SPOILER_PREFERENCE_SELECTED
The player chooses whether sharing conceals the first guess feedback.

### AUTOCOMPLETE_SELECTED
The player chooses whether known correct positions are filled automatically in subsequent guesses.

### SHARE_REQUESTED
The player asks to share a completed result.

### SETTINGS_REQUESTED
The player asks to open the game settings.

### GUIDE_REQUESTED
The player asks to read how to play.

### STATISTICS_REQUESTED
The player asks to view local statistics and any revealed result for today's challenge.

### PANEL_DISMISSED
The player dismisses the open panel using its close action, Escape, or the surrounding backdrop.

### APPEARANCE_SELECTED
The player chooses to follow the device appearance, use a light appearance, or use a dark appearance.

### SELECTION_RETRY_REQUESTED
The player explicitly requests another acquisition of an unavailable current-day challenge.

### STATISTICS_SUMMARY_SELECTED
The player selects the statistics summary in the open statistics panel.

### HISTORY_SELECTED
The player selects the local match history in the open statistics panel.

### HISTORY_MATCH_TOGGLED
The player expands or collapses a concluded match in the local history.
