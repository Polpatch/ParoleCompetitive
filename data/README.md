# Word data and calendar

The upstream snapshot is [napolux/paroleitaliane](https://github.com/napolux/paroleitaliane), revision `2951524b6d5b67986f542937cef16a54af63ff84`, acquired 2026-11-08. The vendored files and their SHA-256 checksums are:

- `paroleitaliane/60000_parole_italiane.txt`: `2d8d2ea8f50eed05de6f0dbf99e07fafdc836889c14429f1cfa689ba762e803e`
- `paroleitaliane/660000_parole_italiane.txt`: `1fc0688a5cc60366d9e849580d1fe9f87fe7bf4c8c996f2d2e640ada3610bc03`

The upstream MIT license and attribution are in `upstream/LICENSE`. Upstream states that the provenance of some lists is unknown. This is not an official, authoritative, exhaustive or legally guaranteed dictionary. The separately licensed CC-BY-SA `coniugazione_verbi.txt`, dedicated proper-name lists and aggregates containing them are not used. No Par🇮🇹le code, artwork, or schedule is used.

Run `node scripts/generate-words.mjs` from the project root. The script unions the three source lists and `additions.txt`, retains exactly five ASCII letters, uppercases, deduplicates and sorts them. Accents are not removed or transliterated. `additions.txt` records the reviewed ordinary Italian words AUDIO and OZONO; both are also present in the pinned 660k source, whose attribution above applies. They were retained as normal Italian words, not input-specific exceptions. `exclusions.txt` records LAZIO, a proper name with no ordinary Italian word meaning, and HELLO, an English greeting rather than an Italian-use loanword; neither was previously allowed. LUIGI is retained because it is also an Italian common noun for a French gold coin ([Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/vocabolario/luigi/)), not exclusively a proper name. The generator refuses exclusions of any previously allowed guess.

The abandoned v2 permutation and its 2026-11-09 cutover are not used. From **2026-11-08**, the daily publisher selects an index in the entire ordered canonical vocabulary. Its vocabulary identity is lowercase SHA-256 hex of the UTF-8 bytes of each uppercase word followed by LF, including the final LF. The generated client vocabulary and identity must match the publication; a vocabulary change requires an explicitly agreed later boundary and compatible client release.

For an absent current Rome date, decode `PAROLE_DAILY_HMAC_KEY` from exactly 64 hexadecimal digits into 32 random bytes. Compute HMAC-SHA256 with that key and only the UTF-8 bytes of `YYYY-MM-DD` as message, without a newline or vocabulary identifier. Interpret all 32 digest bytes as an unsigned big-endian integer and take its remainder modulo the vocabulary length. The same key, date and ordering reproduce the same index; independent daily selections can repeat. The first fixed record always wins over later key or vocabulary changes.

The append-only `daily-data` branch stores one JSON file per fixed date, containing exactly `schema`, `date`, `vocabularyId`, `challengeId`, and `index`. `schema` is 1 and `challengeId` is `hmac-v1:DATE:VOCABULARY_ID`. Only the current record is included in Pages at `daily/current.json`; its index and the public vocabulary reveal the current answer. The branch is durable data only, never a source of executable scripts. An invalid or incompatible existing record stops publication and must not be replaced. A failed deploy does not undo the fixed record; an authorized later run republishes it without needing the key. Future dates and missed historical dates are never generated.

`solutions-v1.txt` is the editorially reviewed, ordered solution list, not an automatically regenerated sample. It retains common Italian nouns, adjectives, inflected forms, verbs and function words (for example ACQUA, BELLO, AMARE and ANCHE); meanings shared with names are retained as common words. The candidates GRECO and RUSSO were excluded as nationality/name ambiguities and PAZZO as potentially stigmatizing; no names, obscure or malformed entries were retained. The full retained list is the record of the review.

Calendar v1 uses epoch 2026-01-01 in the Gregorian calendar of Europe/Rome. Its integer day difference from that epoch, modulo the length of the frozen v1 list (using a nonnegative remainder), selects its zero-based position. Its source list, ordering, epoch and algorithm remain unchanged for dates before 2026-11-08.
