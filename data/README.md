# Word data and calendar

The upstream snapshot is [napolux/paroleitaliane](https://github.com/napolux/paroleitaliane), revision `2951524b6d5b67986f542937cef16a54af63ff84`, acquired 2026-11-08. The vendored source files are `paroleitaliane/1000_parole_italiane_comuni.txt` and `paroleitaliane/60000_parole_italiane.txt`; the upstream MIT license and attribution are in `upstream/LICENSE`. Upstream states that the provenance of some lists is unknown. This is not an official or authoritative dictionary and no legal guarantee is asserted. The separately licensed CC-BY-SA `coniugazione_verbi.txt`, proper-name lists and combined lists containing them are not used. No Par🇮🇹le code, artwork, or schedule is used.

Run `node scripts/generate-words.mjs` from the project root. The script combines the two source lists, retains only entries consisting of exactly five ASCII letters, uppercases, deduplicates, and sorts them to create the bundled allowed-guess list. It checks that every reviewed solution occurs in both the common-word candidates and the resulting dictionary. Accented forms are excluded rather than silently altered.

`solutions-v1.txt` is the editorially reviewed, ordered solution list, not an automatically regenerated sample. It retains common Italian nouns, adjectives, inflected forms, verbs and function words (for example ACQUA, BELLO, AMARE and ANCHE); meanings shared with names are retained as common words. The candidates GRECO and RUSSO were excluded as nationality/name ambiguities and PAZZO as potentially stigmatizing; no names, obscure or malformed entries were retained. The full retained list is the record of the review.

Calendar v1 uses epoch 2026-01-01 in the Gregorian calendar of Europe/Rome. Its integer day difference from that epoch, modulo the length of the frozen v1 list (using a nonnegative remainder), selects its zero-based position. No runtime randomness or network lookup is involved. The v1 source list, ordering, epoch and algorithm are immutable, including for historical dates: new words must use a new schedule with a future effective date, retaining the complete earlier schedule for all dates through the currently published day. Rebuilding from the same files preserves all assignments.
